There was a time when you picked one perfume, wore it for six years, and became known for it. That time is over, darling. In 2026, the girl who smells the best is wearing two or three things at once and refusing to explain herself.

It's called perfume layering, and it's everywhere right now. Warm vanilla, sweet gourmand, a little spice, a body cream underneath. The goal is a scent that is yours alone, which is a very Carrie thing to want and a very affordable thing to do.

I went through Amazon and built a stack of eight scents and layering bases that rate 4.4 stars or higher, with real review counts behind them. Some are viral, some are dupes, and one is a body cream that does half the work for you.

A quick note before we start: scent is personal, so spray before you commit and don't judge a combo until it settles on skin.
