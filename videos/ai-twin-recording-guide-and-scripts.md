# AI twin: recording guide + first 3 presenter scripts

## Part 1: Record your twin footage (do once, ~15 min)
Check the tool's exact requirements first (they differ). Typical:
- **Length:** 2-5 minutes of continuous talking to camera.
- **Camera:** phone, back camera if possible, vertical 9:16 (or the tool's required format), steady on a tripod or stacked books at eye level.
- **Light:** face a window (daylight in front of you, not behind). No overhead shadows.
- **Sound:** quiet room, phone mic 8-12 inches away or a lav mic. No music, no fans.
- **Background:** plain, uncluttered, solid and not matching your clothes.
- **Look:** the hair/makeup/outfit you want your avatar to wear every time. Neutral top (cream or soft brown). Minimal jewelry that jingles.
- **Delivery:** natural, smiling, relaxed hands, look at the lens, pause between sentences, don't rush.
- **Read this script while recording** (covers different mouth shapes): "Hi, I'm Jessica from Looks Like Luxe. Chocolate brown is the shade of fall. Today I'm sharing popular finds on Amazon: a faux leather trench, knee-high boots, a slouchy bag and baggy jorts. Check the size chart, read the reviews, and always compare colors before you buy. Everything is linked in my bio. Thanks for watching!" Repeat with a couple of different energy levels (calm, excited).
- **Consent step:** the tool will ask you to confirm it's your own face/voice. Do it. Do not use anyone else's likeness.
- **Label:** every video gets the platform's "AI-generated" label plus on-screen text.

## Part 3: One-week test plan with a free tier
- Day 1: choose ONE tool (free tier). Upload your footage. Review the twin: does it look/sound like you? If not, re-record.
- Day 2: render Script 1. Add the cutout images as b-roll in CapCut or the tool's editor.
- Day 3: post to TikTok with the AI label + captions. Day 4: post the carousel version. Compare saves and bio clicks.
- Only pay if it clearly beats your faceless videos.
